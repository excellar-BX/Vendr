How it works

Three steps to your
first sale.

No technical knowledge needed. If you have a phone and something to sell, you're ready.

01 — Create
List your item
Snap a photo, set your price, add a description. Your listing goes live in under 60 seconds.
02 — Connect
Chat & agree
Buyers message you directly in-app. Agree on price, delivery, or pickup — no middlemen.
03 — Collect
Get paid safely
Funds are held in escrow, then released to you after the buyer confirms — zero risk of fraud.
Features

Everything you need
to sell with confidence.

Built specifically for West African markets — fast, offline-friendly, and designed for trust.

Vendor Verification
Every vendor goes through identity checks before selling. Buyers always know who they're dealing with.
Escrow Payments
Funds sit safely in escrow until delivery is confirmed. Auto-released after 5 days — zero disputes, full peace of mind.
In-App Chat
Negotiate, agree, and close deals without leaving the app. Chat stays tied to each listing for full context.
Hyperlocal Search
Find listings within walking distance. Set your radius and see what's available right around you.
Seller Analytics
Track views, inquiries, and revenue from your vendor dashboard. Know what sells and when.
Multi-Country Ready
Paystack for Nigeria, Flutterwave for Ghana and broader West Africa — your store, your region.
