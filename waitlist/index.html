The Problem

Great businesses.
Invisible to buyers.

WhatsApp Status isn't a business

Millions of vendors post daily on status and TikTok — getting likes from people who'll never buy. Engagement without conversion is just noise.

No targeted local buyers

A food vendor in Surulere has no way to reach hungry people 500m away. Discovery needs to be proximity-first and intent-first.

Zero trust infrastructure

Buyers fear scams. Vendors fear bad reviews with no recourse. The informal economy has always run on word of mouth — we're building the digital version of that.

Fear kills business ideas

Most would-be entrepreneurs never start because they can't see a path to customers. Vendr is the confidence they need to take the leap.
